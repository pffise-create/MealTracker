## Design Context

### Users
MealTracker is initially a private native iPhone app for Patrick: an adult who tracks calories and macros, repeats many meals, uses Apple Health, accepts editable AI estimates, and is motivated by persistent games and accumulation. The core job is to record every eating occasion in seconds without food judgment or repetitive entry. Adventure is optional motivation entered after logging, never a gate in the logging flow.

### Brand Personality
Confident, vivid, and humane. The experience should feel premium and native, with the composure of a serious habit tool and the intrigue of a high-production fantasy adventure. Copy is concise, adult, and encouraging—never cute, moralizing, clinical, or casino-like.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved Electric Blue direction, Plus Jakarta Sans display type, DM Sans body type, Lucide icon language, light/dark support, and accessible native SwiftUI behavior. Today remains bright and disciplined. Adventure may deepen into an atmospheric dusk-blue world with painterly depth, restrained metallic warmth, map-like texture built from native shapes, and cinematic composition. Avoid generic neon gaming UI, glassmorphism, card grids, purple-blue AI gradients, and childish gamification.

### Design Principles
1. Logging remains faster and more visually dominant than play; Adventure never interrupts it.
2. Reward consistency, not nutrition outcomes. Macro misses never create loss, danger, or shame.
3. Make game choices legible and consequential, with the app—not generated prose—authoritative for state and dice.
4. Use a few cinematic moments and tactile interactions instead of constant decoration; respect Reduce Motion and VoiceOver.
5. Preserve permanent progress and clearly communicate costs before spending banked energy.
